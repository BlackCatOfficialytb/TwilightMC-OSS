# TwilightMC Licensing Policy

TwilightMC follows a **multi-tiered licensing model** designed to benefit the open-source Rust ecosystem while protecting the complete product against unauthorized proprietary exploitation.

---

## 1. Workspace Breakdown

* **Core Utilities & Libraries (`Apache-2.0`):**
  The low-level subsystem crates (`twilight-manifest`, `twilight-downloader`, `twilight-runtime`, `twilight-horizon`) are licensed under the permissive **Apache License, Version 2.0**. You are free to integrate and reuse these crates in your own software, provided you retain copyright notices.

* **Application & Launch Engine (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`):**
  The top-level client applications (`twilight-cli`, `twilight-ui`, `twilight-launch`, `twilight-auth`) are licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPL-3.0-or-later)**. Any distribution, modification, or hosted network deployment of these components must keep the complete source code publicly accessible under the same license terms.

---

## 2. Commercial & Permissive Dual-Licensing

If you are a commercial entity, game studio, or independent developer wishing to use the AGPL-covered components of TwilightMC:
* Without being bound by the copyleft/source-disclosure requirements of the AGPLv3.
* Under a custom permissive, proprietary, or white-label commercial agreement.

You can request an alternate commercial license grant.
