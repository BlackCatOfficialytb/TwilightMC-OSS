Interactions. Clicking a card is selection (panel slides in); double-click or ▶ Launch plays. Right-click context menus are retained — the palette and panel are additions, not replacements.
Risks. The 56px rail needs tooltip QA and a visible focus state on every glyph; the palette must stay scope-limited (instances + actions) or it becomes a second settings page. Context panel meta rows (playtime, mod count) need real data from the scan pass before build.
