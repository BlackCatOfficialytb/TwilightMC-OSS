# Voice & tone — TwilightMC

Operational, terse, honest. The launcher talks like a good terminal: short
lines, present tense, no theater.

## Rules

1. **Sentence case everywhere.** No title case for buttons, tabs, or
   headings. Only uppercase captions (10px, tracked) may shout, and they
   never contain punctuation.
2. **Status is one crisp word.** `Ready`, `Running Fabric 1.21.4…`,
   `Instance deleted.`, `Modpack installed.` Never "Great! Your instance has
   been successfully removed 🎉".
3. **No exclamation marks, no celebration emoji, no marketing voice** inside
   the product. "Get more mods", not "Discover thousands of amazing mods!".
4. **Ask before destroying.** Destructive labels name the object and the
   outcome: "Delete instance", "✕ Delete Permanently", "♻ Move to Trash".
   Confirmations state the consequence ("…and all of its game files?").
5. **Empty states teach one thing.** "No instances yet — Create one from a
   version list, or drop a .mrpack into the wizard." One sentence of why,
   one action.
6. **Errors are strings from the backend, shown verbatim**, prefixed by
   nothing, in rose. They never apologize ("oops") and never joke.
7. **Numbers are plain.** `4.2M` downloads, `12.3 KB` files, `1,024 MiB`
   memory. No precision theater.
8. **Pronouns stay out.** "Open mods folder", not "Open your mods folder".
9. **Pre-release honesty is part of the voice**: amber "pre-release UI"
   badges stay up; changelogs and in-app copy never promise stability.
10. **Ellipsis means working** (`Installing…`, `Running X…`); a finished
    action never ends in one.
